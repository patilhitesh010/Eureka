COMPETITION RULEBOOK

Manual & Guidelines

Read the complete rules governing the event tracks, registrations, and formatting requirements.

Pitch Timing & Q&A

Each team is allotted exactly 7 to 8 minutes to deliver their pitch deck to the judging panel, followed by a 10 to 12 minute Q&A session. Timings are strictly monitored.

Language & Attire

Pitching must be done in English. The Q&A session can be in English or Hindi based on the team's comfort level. Formal business wear is compulsory for all members.

Branding Requirements

All submitted slides must feature the official NEC, E-Cell SIT, and EUREKA partner logos clearly visible on the top or bottom margins.

Roster Size & Leadership

Teams must have a minimum of 1 member and a maximum of 5 members. The designated Team Leader must initiate the sign-up and manage the roster entries.

Student Roster Verification

Open exclusively to SIT Degree and Diploma students. Leaders must enter the correct names, college enrollment numbers, semesters, emails, and phone numbers for all members.

Auto-Save & Editing

Roster registrations continuously auto-save prior to lock. You can add or swap members via your Student Dashboard before the deadline.

PPT & Document Formats

Files must be uploaded in .ppt, .pptx, or .pdf format. Supporting reports or spreadsheets must be in .doc, .docx, or .pdf format. Max file size is 10MB per file.

Digital receipts

Every successful upload triggers a digital receipt on the dashboard. Revision uploads are allowed dynamically prior to the locked final closing date.
